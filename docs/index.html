Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Early Access, privacy, and exports.

Is Lenscribe free during Early Access?

Yes. Paid billing is paused until next year, and every signed-in account automatically receives a complimentary 3-month Pro pass with a live countdown in Pass & Sync.

What happens if I lose internet while scanning or editing?

Lenscribe saves local records and scanned previews into an on-device Room SQLite database. You can restore unsynced items back to the cloud anytime or export/import a portable .json backup.

Can I scan private documents without storing them?

Yes. Enable Zero-Data-Retention Mode inside Pass & Sync to keep documents strictly in memory and skip both Cloud Firestore and local SQLite backup storage.

How do I delete my account and stored scans?

Open Pass & Sync → Privacy Notice & Account Control → Delete Account & All Data to permanently wipe your cloud profile, scan history, workflows, and local SQLite backup database.
