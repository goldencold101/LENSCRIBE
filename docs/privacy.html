Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 • Package: com.aistudio.lenscribe.xqvbmr

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Details: When you sign in with Google, Lenscribe stores your user ID, display name, and email address solely for authentication, document sync, and Early Access quota tracking.
  • Document Captures & OCR Text: Images selected via Android Photo Picker or captured with the in-app camera are sent over encrypted TLS solely to transcribe text and tables at your request. Lenscribe does not request broad device storage permissions.
  • Workflow Rules: If you create automated HTTPS webhooks, only the destination URL and trigger rules you configure are stored.

2. Local SQLite Storage & Zero-Data-Retention Mode

When Zero-Data-Retention Mode is off, transcribed documents and preview thumbnails are stored in your private per-user Firestore collection and mirrored locally inside an app-private SQLite database for offline access and portable .json export/restore.

When Zero-Data-Retention Mode is turned ON in Pass & Sync, scans remain strictly in memory for copying or exporting and are skipped from both Cloud Firestore and local SQLite databases.

3. Data Sharing & Security

Lenscribe contains no third-party advertising networks and never sells your data. All network communications enforce HTTPS/TLS, and user-configured webhooks block private/internal IP targets.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual documents in the Library tab, clear local SQLite backups using Clear Local Backup DB, or permanently delete your account and all cloud/local data via Pass & Sync → Delete Account & All Data.
